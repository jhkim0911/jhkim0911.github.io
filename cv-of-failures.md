## CV of Failures

Here is the story: I recently came across a “CV of failures” written by a student in our lab, and it made me think about how much of a researcher’s life is built on rejection (including mine!!) and how true this is at every career stage. Papers, grants, jobs, fellowships, and ideas that never worked out. Resilience is not really optional in this career. It is something we learn through repetition. Before my memory becomes too selective, I wanted to write down some of these moments for myself.

Every day on LinkedIn and X, we come across announcements of accepted papers, new positions, awards, and other milestones. At a recent conference, I happened to meet another postdoc who told me that, from the outside, it looked like things had been going really well for me. I appreciated that, but it also reminded me how little we see of what happens behind those announcements. We mostly see each other’s successes, not the rejections, uncertainty, and failed attempts that came before them. If someone happens to come across this page while dealing with their own rejection, I hope it can be a small reminder that there is usually much more behind a CV than what makes it onto the front page.
